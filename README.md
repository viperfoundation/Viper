# Viper

![viper logo](/viper-logo.jpg)

**Viper** – a private messenger built on the decentralized transport layer of TON (ADNL protocol). 

It uses a relay server to forward messages between participants globally.

## Highlights

🔹 **Direct, device to device** – messages and files go straight between phones whenever possible, with no server in the middle.

🔹 **TON Drop: chat with no internet** – people nearby find each other over Bluetooth and talk with no network at all. Like AirDrop, but distance doesn't have to end the conversation: once you've met, the chat keeps going over TON.

🔹 **End-to-end encrypted** – only you and your peer can read your messages, over every route.

🔹 **History lives on your device** – the relay deletes a message as soon as it's delivered.

🔹 **No phone number or email** – your crypto key is your account. Start with a mnemonic phrase, no sign-up.

🔹 **Voice notes, video circles and large files** – transfers pick up where they left off if the connection drops.

🔹 **.ton usernames** – use a TON DNS domain you own on-chain as your name.

🔹 **Built-in TON wallet** – a non-custodial wallet to send and receive TON-based assets right inside the messenger.

🔹 **Viper Pro** – Anon Mode hides you from search, and you can hide your online status.

🔹 **Simple and fast** – lightweight, distraction-free apps for iOS and macOS.
